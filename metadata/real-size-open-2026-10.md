# Works still without a size, 13 October 2026

143 of the 562 works that had no size on 13 October 2026 still have none.
The pass is described in [real-size-fixes-2026-10.md](real-size-fixes-2026-10.md).
Each line gives the work, its id without the `collection-of-beauty-`
prefix, and what the search found. A figure named here is a lead, not a
source: none of them is stored.

The research ran on one shared allowance of 200 web searches, which ran
out about two thirds of the way through every batch. Works marked as not
searched further are the first to try again. The same holds for the
records behind a bot check, which a person can open in a browser:
honolulu.emuseum.com (5 works), britishmuseum.org, catalog.shm.ru,
dorotheum.com, blanton.emuseum.com, collections.louvre.fr and the Musei
Civici di Venezia catalogue. goskatalog.ru did not answer from this
network at all (5 works).
