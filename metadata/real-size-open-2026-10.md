# Works still without a size, 13 October 2026

103 of the 4,555 works have no size. They were 562 on the morning of
13 October 2026. A first pass that day found 419 sizes and a second
found 40; both are described in
[real-size-fixes-2026-10.md](real-size-fixes-2026-10.md).

Each line gives the work, its id without the `collection-of-beauty-`
prefix, and what the search found. A figure named here is a lead, not a
source: none of them is stored. Figures in quotation marks are a
record's own words, in its own order.

## What a person can still do

One record sits behind a challenge that needs a person:

- Hans Holbein the Younger, Head of a Woman
  (`hans-holbein-d-j-head-of-a-woman-wga11590`): a drawing in the
  Louvre. `collections.louvre.fr` shows an "I am not a robot" box in
  Chrome too. Search it for Holbein, "Tête de femme", and read the
  size.

Seven works have a figure, and whether its source is enough is a
decision:

- Kim Hong-do, Samgongbulhwando (`kim-hong-do-samgongbulhwando`): the
  Encyclopedia of Korean Culture gives "세로 133㎝, 가로 418㎝", which
  fits the scan. The Korea Heritage Service record of Treasure 2000 and
  the Leeum collection state no size. An encyclopedia is not a record.
- Léon Bakst, Sleeping Beauty, scene 4: The Awakening
  (`sleeping-beauty-by-l-bakst-04`): Schouvaloff's catalogue of the
  Thyssen-Bornemisza Collection (1987), cat. 12, "Pencil and
  watercolour 48 x 66.8cm". It was read in the Internet Archive's full
  text, the plate was not seen, and the scan is 9% narrower than that
  sheet. Bakst repeated his designs.
- Ivan Aivazovsky, Passage of the Jews through the Red Sea
  (`aivazovsky-passage-of-the-jews-through-the-red-sea`): the Tretyakov
  Gallery Magazine, no. 4 2016, captions the same picture "96 × 160.
  Частное собрание, США". It is a caption in an essay about a picture
  in private hands, and the scan is 8% wider.
- Boris Kustodiev, The Blue House (`kustodiev-goluboydomik`): held by
  Peterhof, as the museum's own news says. The Tretyakov Gallery
  Magazine (2004) gives "97×80"; the scan and the journal's own
  illustration are both near 0.74 wide to 1 high, the figure is 0.82.
- John Byam Liston Shaw, Now Is the Pilgrim Year Fair Autumn's Charge
  (`shaw-john-byam-liston-now-is-pilgrim-fair-autumn-s-charge`): the
  dealer's page says "120.00cm wide 88.30cm high"; the Victorian Web
  quotes the same dealer with "33 1/2 x 47 inches, 85 x 119.5
  centimetres".
- Louis-Léopold Boilly, Two Young Friends Kissing
  (`deux-jeunes-amies-qui-s-embrassent-de-l-l-boilly-musee-cognacq-jay-paris-51992741281`):
  three figures 3 cm apart. The Cognacq-Jay press kit says 42,5 × 35, a
  blog's copy of the National Gallery's caption 45.5 × 37.5, and
  Christie's London, 13 December 1996, lot 65, 45.7 × 37.8.
- Louis-Léopold Boilly, The Barrel Game
  (`louis-leopold-boilly-the-barrel-game`): 37.8 × 46.8 cm fits the
  scan. It is the National Gallery's caption for its exhibition of
  2019, read only as a blog reprints it. The Art UK story on the
  exhibition does not print it.

### The scan is a detail, a crop, or shows more than the work (15)

The whole work's size is known for seven of them. It is not stored: the
hang sizes a work from the long edge of the stored size, so a detail
would hang as large as the whole painting.

- Claude Monet, Antibes (`p-1948-sc-276-scaled-aspect-ratio-16-9-3-scaled`): whole work 92.4 × 65.5 cm, The Courtauld Gallery, P.1948.SC.276. The file is a 16:9 crop made for the Courtauld's website.
- Dong Yuan, Xiao and Xiang Rivers (`dong-yuan-rivers-detail`): whole work 141.4 × 50 cm, Palace Museum, Beijing, 新00147099. Commons names the file a detail.
- Edgar Degas, Degas (`degas-deux-danseuses`): whole work 46.04 × 66.68 cm, Shelburne Museum, 1972-69.6. The file is the banner of the Shelburne's Impressionist Paintings page, twice as wide as high.
- Eduard Gaertner, Panorama of Berlin (`eduard-gartner-002`): whole work 110 × 91 cm, SPSG, Schloss Charlottenburg, GK I 6179. The Yorck Project scan is the right half.
- Giovanni Bellini, Crucifixion in a Jewish Cemetery (`giovanni-bellini-crocifissione-in-un-cimitero-ebraico-crocifisso-niccolini-da-camugliano-1480-85-ca`): a 3:2 photograph of part of an upright panel. No citable size for the whole work either.
- Hans Holbein the Younger, Bad-war (`bad-war`): whole work 95.8 × 27.6 cm, Albertina, Inv. 17243, an anonymous copy after Holbein. The file is a crop of that sheet.
- Michelangelo Buonarroti, Separation of the Earth from the Waters (`michelangelo-separation-of-the-earth-from-the-waters-00`): one bay of the Sistine ceiling with its ignudi and medallions. No citable size for the scene.
- Philipp Otto Runge, The Great Morning (`philipp-otto-runge-the-great-morning-detail-wga20518`): whole work 113 × 152 cm, Hamburger Kunsthalle, HK-1022. Commons and the Web Gallery of Art call the file a detail.
- Charles Le Brun, Anger (`charles-le-brun-la-colere`): a crop of the Wellcome's 32625i, Hebert after Le Brun, "La Colere. Anger", no. 18, about 1770. Whole plate: "platemark 24.7 x 20 cm". The catalogue calls it a drawing of 1668.
- Pierre-Joseph Redouté, Centifolia Rose, Anemone and Clematis (`redoute-flowers01`): a bouquet cut out on white, from "Rose, Anémone, Clématide" in Choix des plus belles fleurs. No record of the plate was found.
- Annibale Carracci, Jupiter and Juno (`jupiter-and-juno-annibale-carracci-fragment`): a visitor's photograph of part of the Farnese ceiling.
- Raphael, The School of Athens (`escola-de-atenas-vaticano-2`): a visitor's photograph of the whole wall from below, with the vault corners and the door head in it and the bottom strip missing. The Vatican's catalogue holds photo-archive records only.
- Tsukioka Yoshitoshi, Adachi Moor (`adachi-moor`): a photograph of the uploader's own impression on a black ground; the print fills about 69% of the width and 87% of the height. LACMA M.84.31.138a-b, another impression: sheet 74.4 × 24.1 cm.
- Ernst Ludwig Kirchner, Female Nude with Foliage Shadows (`female-nude-with-foliage-shadows`): the photograph shows the picture in its frame; the board fills about three fifths of the width. Kirchner Museum Davos has no online record.
- Tsuchiya Kōitsu, Chinese Warship Ting Yuang Visiting Japan (`chinese-warship-ting-yuang-visiting-japan`): a dealer's photograph of three loose sheets with gaps on a grey ground. No museum record of the triptych was found.
