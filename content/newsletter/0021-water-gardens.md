At Argenteuil, a boat still gives the water a subject. At Montgeron, trees and reflections spread across the panel.

In the Giverny pond the horizon drops away. Venice gives Monet buildings, but he keeps returning them to water. The late lilies hold grasses, pads and sky on one surface.

Whistler's Thames in [Candlelight and night](/newsletter/0010-candlelight-and-night) nearly disappeared into tone. Monet keeps adding colour until reflection becomes the whole room.
