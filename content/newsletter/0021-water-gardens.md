---

At Argenteuil the boat still gives the water something to reflect. At Montgeron the trees double cleanly into the panel.

By Giverny the horizon is gone and the lily pads sit on the same plane as the reflected sky. Whistler's Thames in [Candlelight and night](/newsletter/0010-candlelight-and-night) almost disappeared into tone; Monet keeps adding colour until the pond holds grass, pad and sky in one vertical field.

Venice was a single autumn trip in 1908. The Salute keeps rising at the end of the Grand Canal, but Monet works it back into pink and turquoise reflections on the water.
