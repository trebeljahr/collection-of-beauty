Vesalius gives the skeleton a landscape. The hand engraving removes the person and keeps the mechanism.

Blake bends Newton over a diagram. Vermeer puts dividers, chart and globe in one quiet room. Wright makes a flask shine like a small altar.

The air-pump crowd in [Candlelight and night](/newsletter/0010-candlelight-and-night) watched science as theatre. These tables keep the instruments closer to the hand.
