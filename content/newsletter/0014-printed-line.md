Schongauer's demons are made from line before they are made from fear. Each claw and wing has its own cut.

Durer packs tools and figures into engraved darkness. Holbein keeps death small enough to fit in a book. Rembrandt lets a windmill stand in open paper.

The [aquatint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquatint) birds in the next century will need tone. These prints trust line first.
