The Acanthometra plate looks like a cabinet of stars. Each form is tiny, but the page gives every spine a place.

Diatoms turn into glass ornament. A jellyfish carries a personal name. Glass sponges become baskets. Hummingbirds move the method from the microscope to the branch.

Haeckel's plates answer the flower studies in [Flowers and branches](/newsletter/0007-flowers-and-branches) with a colder kind of abundance.
