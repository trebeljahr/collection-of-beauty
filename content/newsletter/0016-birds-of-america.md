The flamingo folds itself into the sheet. The heron does the same, neck and legs bent to obey the paper.

The hummingbirds solve the page by multiplying. The owl solves it by facing forward. The Carolina parakeets fill the branch with colour that no longer belongs to a living North American flock.

Haeckel's specimens in [Microscopic ornament](/newsletter/0013-microscopic-ornament) were arranged by form. Audubon keeps the form life-size when he can.
