---

Winter is the great equalizer of village life. When the canal freezes, gentry, peasants, dogs, and birds all end up on the same white floor. Painters figured out a long time ago that ice is a stage.

So this issue is mostly stages. Hiroshige buries a village in snow it never actually gets. Bruegel runs games and a bird trap on the same sheet of ice and lets you draw your own conclusions. Avercamp fills a canal with a small town's worth of gossip. Rembrandt empties it back out to one stopped sledge, and Friedrich closes with a procession, a coffin, and a ruin.

Same season, opposite silences.
