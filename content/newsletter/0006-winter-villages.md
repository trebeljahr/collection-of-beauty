Bruegel's village has a trap on one side and skaters on the other. The ice turns work, play and danger into one flat public floor.

Avercamp keeps adding figures until the canal feels like a street. Rembrandt thins the scene to a few marks and a sledge. Guo's boats move through a different winter, all rigging and ink line.

Friedrich's abbey has no village bustle. The snow lies on stone, trunks and the path to the ruined door.
