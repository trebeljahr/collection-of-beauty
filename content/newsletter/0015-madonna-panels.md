Rogier gives the Child a book. Giorgione gives the Virgin a high throne. Cranach gives her a fir tree.

Parmigianino makes the body long enough to unsettle the chapel. Murillo brings the image down to a child, a father and a small bird.

The gold grounds in [Flowers and branches](/newsletter/0007-flowers-and-branches) made branches shine. These panels use setting to change the scale of devotion.
