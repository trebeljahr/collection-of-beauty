Bosch crowds the garden until it cannot behave like a real place. Runge brings the scale down to children and leaves.

Rubens builds a garden from stone, courtship and sculpture. Hiroshige cuts the view with iris stalks. Monet raises the horizon until the garden looks staged against the sea.

After Monet's pond in [Water gardens](/newsletter/0021-water-gardens), these gardens have edges again.
