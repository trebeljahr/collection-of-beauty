Painting music is an impossible problem: the one thing the picture is about is the one thing it cannot contain. So painters paint everything around the sound: the tuning, the listening, the faces. Somehow you hear it anyway.

Titian's meadow concert is pure mood. Grünewald's angels play for patients who needed the colours as much as the music. Caravaggio paints the moment before. La Tour paints what musicians do when the music stops paying, and Menzel reconstructs one candlelit evening from a century's distance.

Five rooms, five silences, all of them somehow loud.
