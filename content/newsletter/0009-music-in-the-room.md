Caravaggio's musicians have not quite begun. One boy tunes, one reads, one waits with his mouth open. The room is narrow and full of shoulders.

Titian sets music outside. Grunewald puts it inside an opened altarpiece. La Tour turns a hurdy-gurdy into a street quarrel. Menzel makes a court room face one flute.

Like the readers in [Books and reading](/newsletter/0004-books-and-reading), these figures are caught by an object held close to the body.
