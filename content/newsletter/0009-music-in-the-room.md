Caravaggio's musicians have not begun to play. The boy at the front bends over a tuning lute, another peers at a sheet of music, a third holds his cornetto with the mouthpiece still on his lap.

Titian sets music in an open pastoral. Grünewald packs it into the opened second view of a hospital altarpiece. La Tour turns a hurdy-gurdy into a knife fight.

Menzel reconstructs the Sanssouci music room a hundred years after the event, down to the candle sconces and the chairs, and gives a king a flute small enough to disappear into his hand.
