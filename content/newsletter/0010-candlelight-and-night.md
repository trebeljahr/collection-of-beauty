Harunobu's lantern is small enough to hold. It makes a few blossoms visible and leaves the rest of the night alone.

La Tour's candle presses two figures together. Wright hides his lamp inside science. Grimshaw doubles gaslight on wet stone. Whistler leaves only dim intervals over the Thames.

The mirrors in [Mirrors and reflections](/newsletter/0008-mirrors-and-reflections) return a face. Night returns a flame.
