Harunobu's lantern is small enough to hold. It lights one branch in snow and leaves the rest of the night where it was.

La Tour presses two figures together over one wick. Wright hides his candle behind a bowl of water and an experiment. Grimshaw doubles every gas lamp in wet stone. Whistler dims the Thames until only the lights remain.

The mirrors in [Mirrors and reflections](/newsletter/0008-mirrors-and-reflections) returned a face. Night returns a flame.
