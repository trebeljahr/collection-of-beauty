---

Mantegna ties the saint to a broken classical column and leaves the ancient city in pieces behind him. Rubens lets Palatine walls share their hillside with herds and grazing land.

Turner reflects an abbey in still water until the ruin is twice its surviving height. Schinkel invents a Gothic cathedral on a sea cliff and lets the rock do the work of a plinth.

The bridges in [Bridges and crossings](/newsletter/0005-bridges-and-crossings) carried people across water. Friedrich opens a cemetery gate halfway and leaves the iron unlatched over a path that runs into mist.
