Mantegna ties the saint to a broken column. Rubens lets Palatine walls share space with animals. Turner puts an abbey beside water and lets the reflection finish it.

Schinkel invents a church on a rock. Friedrich opens a cemetery gate and stops at the path.

The bridges in [Bridges and crossings](/newsletter/0005-bridges-and-crossings) carried people across water. These thresholds hold people at the edge.
