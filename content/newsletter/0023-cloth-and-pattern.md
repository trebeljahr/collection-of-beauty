---

Kaigetsudo lets the robe make the body. Van Dyck turns a parasol into rank. Delacroix fills a room with patterned surfaces.

Vigee Le Brun paints diplomacy through silk. Goyo keeps the scene quiet, one blue kimono and a comb moving through hair.

The mirrors in [Mirrors and reflections](/newsletter/0008-mirrors-and-reflections) repeated bodies. Cloth changes their edges.
