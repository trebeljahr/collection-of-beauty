Kaigetsudō lets the patterned robe make the body. Van Dyck turns a red parasol into rank above a Genoese terrace.

The mirrors in [Mirrors and reflections](/newsletter/0008-mirrors-and-reflections) repeated bodies as image; here cloth changes their edges instead. Delacroix slows the Algiers room down with slippers, hookah and folded cloth. Vigée Le Brun paints a diplomatic visit through silk and a sabre.

Goyō keeps the picture almost still: one blue summer kimono, one tortoiseshell comb, dark hair loose down the back.
