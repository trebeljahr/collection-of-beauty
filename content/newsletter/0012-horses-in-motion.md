Uccello covers the ground with broken lances. The horses are fighters, but they are also pieces in a diagram.

Titian slows the horse into imperial portraiture. Rubens tangles the hunt. Gericault lets the animal rise under a soldier. Kuniyoshi sends two riders straight into the current.

The bridges in [Bridges and crossings](/newsletter/0005-bridges-and-crossings) offered a path over water. Here the horse has to make the path.
