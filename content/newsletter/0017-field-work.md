Schongauer's family walks with bundles. Turner's reapers bend across a field. Rubens puts timber on a wagon and lets the track carry it.

Van Gogh's shepherdess stands still inside the flock. Hokusai's tea field is all rows, workers and the distant triangle of Fuji.

The horse pictures in [Horses in motion](/newsletter/0012-horses-in-motion) used animals for speed and ceremony. Here animals and people move at work pace.
