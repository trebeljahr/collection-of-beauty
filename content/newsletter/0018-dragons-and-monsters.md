---

Chen Rong's dragons never stand still long enough to become outlines. The ink makes a head, a claw, a twist of cloud and then opens again.

Uccello leashes the dragon before the kill. Bosch lets monsters spread across three panels until the saint is barely visible. Veronese's circle keeps the sea beast under the water. Fuseli ties horse, rider and serpent into one knot.

The printed demons in [Printed line](/newsletter/0014-printed-line) were made by Schongauer's burin. These monsters arrive by splashed ink, oil on panel and nineteenth-century Academy darkness.
