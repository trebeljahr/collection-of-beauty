Chen Rong's dragons do not stand still long enough to become outlines. Ink makes a claw, a head, a twist of cloud, then opens again.

Uccello leashes the dragon. Bosch multiplies monsters until the panels swarm. Veronese keeps the sea beast low in the water. Fuseli tightens the serpent around horse and rider.

The printed demons in [Printed line](/newsletter/0014-printed-line) were made by cuts. These monsters arrive by ink wash, panel, oil and dream.
