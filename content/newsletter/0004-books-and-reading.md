---

Someone reading is strangely magnetic to paint and to look at. The reader is the one person in the picture who is somewhere else entirely, and the painting reorganizes itself around that absence.

The five here watch that one act change costume across five centuries. Botticelli and Caravaggio make reading heavy, with all the architecture and skulls. Vermeer turns a letter into a sealed room inside a room. Caillebotte lets reading relax into garden shade. By the time we get to de Blaas the news is being read aloud over the laundry, and privacy is no longer the point.

A few marks on paper can pull a person clean out of the room they are standing in. Which, for what it's worth, is what you are doing right now.
