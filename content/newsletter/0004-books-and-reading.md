Jerome is often shown as a learned hermit surrounded by attributes. Caravaggio keeps only the things needed to make thought feel mortal."

---

Books and letters change the temperature of a picture. A room can be crowded with furniture, fruit or sunlight, but once someone starts reading, the painting begins to organize itself around attention.

Botticelli and Caravaggio make reading strenuous, almost bodily work. Vermeer turns a letter into private theatre. Caillebotte moves the reader into garden shade, and de Blaas lets a message leave privacy altogether, becoming news shared aloud.
